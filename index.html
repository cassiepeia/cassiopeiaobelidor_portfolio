Competitive Analysis

We looked at several potential competing digital banks and traditional fintech apps. The majority of the features between competitors were very similar; however, the main differences we noticed were:

Accessibility Easily Accessible vs. Hardly Accessible onboarding
Interactions Too Many Screens vs. Simplified Linear Interaction
Interface Focus Distracting Graphics vs. Minimalist Financial UI
Verification Multi-day approval vs. Instant Biometric e-KYC

Preparing the Journey

We constructed a user flow of what a basic start-to-finish journey looks like when registering and authenticating in the app. This helped us understand ways users can interact with the product, as well as navigate through primary and secondary user goals:
